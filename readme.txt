Embed your Nivon chatbot on any WordPress site.

== Description ==

An AI support agent for your website. Train it on your own content, embed it on your site, and let it handle customer questions, lead capture, and support around the clock.

With this plugin, you can embed your [Nivon](https://nivon.ai/) chatbot directly into your WordPress site.

This plugin uses Nivon services to run a trained chatbot on your website, based on the data you added in the Nivon app. For details, see our [privacy policy](https://nivon.ai/privacy) and [terms of service](https://nivon.ai/terms).

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How do I connect my chatbot? =

1. Install this plugin on your WordPress website.
2. Copy your publish key and chatbot ID from the Connect to website tab in Nivon.
3. Go to the "Nivon Options" page under the "Settings" menu in your WordPress dashboard.
4. Paste both values into the fields.
5. Click "Save Changes".

= The widget is not showing up =

Check the allowed domains list for your chatbot in Nivon. If your site domain is not on that list, the widget will not load.
